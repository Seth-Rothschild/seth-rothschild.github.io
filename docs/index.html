Summary

I turn loosely defined, ambitious ideas into working software.

I am a Senior Principal Software Engineer in the Dell Technologies Office of the CTO. My recent focus is agentic AI and how developers should work alongside it, including how to raise the engineering bar with extreme programming.

Prior to working at Dell I was a Research Scientist at Feature Labs, where I worked on the open source project Featuretools.

Before that I completed a Ph.D. in mathematics in 2017 under George McNinch at Tufts University, studying unipotent algebraic groups over finite fields of positive characteristic.
